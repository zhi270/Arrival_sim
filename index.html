Personal Voice Mode

Microphone is off. Browser speech recognition is optional; use the command box if mic access is unavailable.
Commands are logged before execution. Unrecognized or incomplete commands are rejected without changing the aircraft.
Supported: “climb and maintain 3000”, “descend and maintain 2000”, “maintain 3000”, “turn left/right heading 270”, “reduce speed to 140”, “say position/heading/altitude/intentions/all”, “select [callsign]”, “[callsign] turn right heading 270”, “start simulator”, “pause/resume”.
Shortcuts: 1 climb · 2 descend · 3 maintain · 4 left · 5 right · 6 speed · P position · H heading · A altitude · I intentions · Space pause.
